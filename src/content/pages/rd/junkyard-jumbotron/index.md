The [Junkyard Jumbotron](http://jumbotron.media.mit.edu/) lets you take a bunch of random displays and instantly stitch them together into a large, virtual display, simply by taking a photograph of them. It works with laptops, smartphones, tablets—anything that runs a web browser. It also highlights a new way of connecting a large number of heterogenous devices to each other in the field, on an ad-hoc basis.

With [Rick Borovoy](http://civic.mit.edu/team/rick-borovoy) at the [MIT Media Lab](http://www.media.mit.edu/).
