This interactive touch table at the [American Society of Radiologic Technologists](http://www.asrt.org/) Museum lets visitors explore the many kinds of radiologic imaging in a fun and engaging way: by assembling a human body from a mixture of images. Working together, users drag individual radiologic images (x-rays, CT scans, MRIs, etc.) onto an outline of a body to create their own vision of Homo radiologicus.

*Body Assembly* was designed by [Richard Lewis Media Group](http://www.rlmg.com) who asked me to develop the interactive.
