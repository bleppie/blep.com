This interactive touch table at the [American Society of Radiologic Technologists](http://www.asrt.org/) Museum lets multiple users explore the post-World War II history of radiologic technologies and the RT profession. Events are grouped into six categories: diagnostic radiography, special procedures/cardiovascular intervention, sonography, MRI, CT, and nuclear medicine.

[Richard Lewis Media Group](http://www.rlmg.org) designed this interactive and asked me to help develop it.
