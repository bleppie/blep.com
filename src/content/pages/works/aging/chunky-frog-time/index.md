*Chunky Frog Time* is a generative art commissioned by Boston Cyberarts for the Boston Harbor Islands Welcome Center located on Boston's Rose Fitzgerald Kennedy Greenway.

Swimming across an ever changing landscape of made-made patterns, a frog's cycling represents the ebb and flow on the islands, as well as the relationship between man, nature and man's concept of nature.
