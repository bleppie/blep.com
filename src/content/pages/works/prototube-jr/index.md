In *Prototube Jr,* a vertical row of LEDs spins around a central axis. At high speeds, the LEDs form the illusion of a cylinder of light. The light patterns are organic and inviting, but the spinning hardware is quite scary.

In collaboration with [JackBackRack](http://www.ai.mit.edu/~jrb/), who conceived of the project, and Jeff Weber, who designed and built the physical device. I designed and programmed the organic, ever-changing patterns.
