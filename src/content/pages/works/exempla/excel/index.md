A stream of creatures circles a glowing sphere, each in a downward spiral getting closer and closer to the glow's boundary. Eventually they are engulfed, perhaps to die, perhaps to transcend the cycles they've been in. With a foot pedal, visits can expand the sphere and accelerate the creatures' passing.
