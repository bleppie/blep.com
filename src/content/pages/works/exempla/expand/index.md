Small creatures mill about the bottom of a darkened wall, aimlessly wandering until a visitor activates a bright spotlight. The creatures race toward the focus of the light, jostling for entry into its brightest part. One at a time they enter, distend, are ejected, and slowly descend only to deflate and restart their journey.
