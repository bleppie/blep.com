Nematodes (*Caenorhabditis elegans*) explore, eat and otherwise interact with microorganisms cultured from my fingers and hair. These various bacteria, fungi, and archaea live on and inside our bodies. There are over 1000 species of bacteria that live in and on us, and there are at least 10 times as many bacterial cells as human cells in each of our bodies.
