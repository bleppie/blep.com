*Flower Revealed* is a companion piece to [Flower Umbilicus](/works/flowers/flower-umbilicus/ "Flower Umbilicus"). It reveals the underside of the earlier piece to viewers. The shapes shift from slow growth to rapid generation, and are drawn to the wounds created by others. An off-center hole, the underside of the *umbilicus*, always remains clear.
