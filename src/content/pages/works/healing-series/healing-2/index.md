Wounds made to *Healing 2* slowly melt down to their essences and eventually disappear, removing all traces of visitor interaction. Circular wounds remain the longest. The inside tries heal the wound by expanding while the outside pushes inwards. The outside eventually wins, and the circle shrinks to a dot and dissolves.
