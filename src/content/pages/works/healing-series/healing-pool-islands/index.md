A version of [Healing Pool](/works/healing-series/healing-pool/ "Healing Pool") that contains large, circular areas that heal differently from the rest of the piece. Wounds made on these 'islands' melt away to nothing, leaving behind no traces. They create a space of relative calm where visitors can slow down and contemplate.
