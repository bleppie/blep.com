*Healing 3* is the only piece in the [Healing Series](/works/healing-series/ "Healing Series") where shapes can spontaneously generate in the wounded area; the other pieces can only grow existing shapes. Dots erupt into existance, aggresively pushing visitors away and covering wounds.

Like [Healing 1](/works/healing-series/healing-1/ "Healing 1"), this piece never grows back the same way. It is permanently changed by every interaction.
