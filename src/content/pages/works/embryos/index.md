Excerpt from the production of 40,000 unique sculptural forms. An exploration of fertility and lack of control, each 'embryo' is grown in a simulation of a vat of chemicals. Randomness and variability ensure that each is unique. Whatever grows is 3D-printed in stoneware and left unglazed.
