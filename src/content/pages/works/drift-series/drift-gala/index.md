A site-specific version of [Drift](/works/drift-series/drift/ "Drift") commissioned by the Boston Society of Architects for the 2005 Build Boston Gala. The drifting panels fit exactly into the frames of the existing windows and could be seen on both sides, to the revelers inside and the passersby outside.
